# Repair Shop Journal: user guide

## First setup
In **Settings** enter the shop name, phone, address, currency and (optionally) a logo,
add your technicians and press **Save settings**. These details appear on receipts and certificates.

## Receiving a device
1. In **Orders** press **New order**.
2. Type the phone number: if the client exists, the name and last device are filled in automatically.
3. Enter the device type, brand, model, serial number, password (stored hidden), accessories and the problem.
4. After saving, an intake receipt is produced as a PDF (A5 or A4, with a QR code).

## Repair workflow
- Status in the right panel: Received, Diagnosis, Waiting for parts, Ready, Delivered.
- Enter the diagnosis, work done, parts (name, quantity, price), labor and prepayment.
  The total and amount due are calculated automatically.
- **SMS text** copies a ready message for the client.
- **Act** produces the completion certificate (with the warranty period) as a PDF.
- PDF files are saved to `Documents\Servis jurnali` and opened automatically.

## Search
The search box matches phone, name, serial number, model or order number.
Filter the list by status or pick “Overdue”.

## Dashboard
Orders in progress, ready but not collected, overdue, revenue this month and a 12-month chart.
Double-click an order in a list to open it.

## Backup and export
- **Export to Excel**: the current list to an .xlsx file.
- **Settings > Back up database**: a copy of the database. The last 10 automatic copies are kept on exit.
